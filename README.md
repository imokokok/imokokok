## Insight

**Before an agent or application acts, verify what it is about to trust.**

[Insight](https://www.oracleinsight.xyz/) provides cross-source oracle
transparency, risk assessments, and signed decision-input evidence for DeFi
applications and AI agents.

[Website](https://www.oracleinsight.xyz/) ·
[MCP & API](https://www.oracleinsight.xyz/ai) ·
[Guard SDK](https://github.com/imokokok/Insight/tree/main/sdk) ·
[Independent verifier](https://github.com/imokokok/Insight/tree/main/verifier)

## PriorSeal

**Establish what was approved, what was observed, and what another party can verify.**

[PriorSeal](https://priorseal.xyz/) binds time-bounded, principal-signed
authorization to observed EVM or Solana execution in a portable receipt.

[Console](https://priorseal.xyz/app) ·
[MCP guide](https://priorseal.xyz/integrations/mcp) ·
[TypeScript SDK](https://github.com/imokokok/PriorSeal/tree/main/sdk)
